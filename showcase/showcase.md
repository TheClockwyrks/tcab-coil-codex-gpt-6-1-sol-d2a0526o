# Coil

A bright serpent. A dark grid. One more core within reach. Coil is a quick game of route planning, sharp turns, and knowing when to leave yourself an escape.

Steer the mint coil toward pink cores. Each bite adds a cell to your tail, turning yesterday's clear path into tomorrow's obstacle. The walls never move, and neither they nor your own body forgive a wrong turn.

Keep eating before the combo bar empties to climb from x1 to x5. The best runs connect one core to the next with purpose: a short route means more points, but a greedy turn can cost the whole round.

Take a breath with pause, then pick up exactly where you left off. Chase your session best with the arrow keys, WASD, or touch controls. The grid is waiting.
